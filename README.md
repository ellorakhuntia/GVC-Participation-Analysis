# GVC Participation Analysis

## Overview

This repository contains the Stata code, documentation, and reproducible workflow developed for my Master's research project at the Centre for Development Studies (CDS), JNU.

The project examines the impact of Global Value Chain (GVC) participation on labour market outcomes among Indian manufacturing firms using pooled World Bank Enterprise Survey (WBES) data for 2014 and 2022. The analysis focuses on whether firms participating in GVCs differ from non-participating firms in terms of employment, labour costs, workforce composition, and gender outcomes.

## Research Question

**Does participation in Global Value Chains lead to significantly different labour market outcomes in Indian firms compared to non-participating firms?**

## Data

**Source:** World Bank Enterprise Survey (WBES), India

- Survey years: 2014 and 2022
- Pooled sample: 18,657 firms
- Firm-level manufacturing survey data

> **Note:** The original WBES dataset is **not included** in this repository because it is distributed under the World Bank's data access and licensing terms.

## Methodology

The empirical workflow consists of:

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Construction of the GVC participation indicator
- Propensity Score Matching (PSM)
- Balance diagnostics
- Average Treatment Effect on the Treated (ATT)
- Statistical comparison using t-tests
